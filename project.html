Northbay Studio

Portfolio / Northbay Studio

Northbay Studio came to me with a five-year-old agency site that still listed a mobile number as their "mobile-friendly" plan — it didn't resize at all below 1024px, and their own case studies were the thing suffering most from it.

The brief

Keep the agency's existing brand (the navy-and-white palette and the condensed headline type were both working well) but rebuild the site itself: a real responsive grid, a case-study template their team could reuse without calling me every time, and a contact form that actually reached an inbox.

What changed

A new one-page-per-case-study template replaced a single "portfolio" gallery page with thumbnails and no detail; a simplified three-step contact form cut submissions-with-missing-info roughly in half in the first month; and the whole site now runs on Bootstrap 5 with no jQuery, so page weight dropped by about a third.

Result

Northbay now publishes a new case study themselves every few weeks instead of asking me to do it, and their own client-facing metrics (time on the case-study pages, contact-form completions) are both up since launch.
